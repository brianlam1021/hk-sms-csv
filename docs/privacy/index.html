私隱政策

生效日期：2026年10月6日（2026-10-06）

資料怎樣處理

本站是靜態網頁。你在各工具輸入的短訊、姓名、金額、帳單、單位資料等，都在你的瀏覽器內運算。我們沒有後端應用程式去接收或儲存這些內容。

本機儲存（localStorage）

部分工具可選擇把草稿或語言偏好寫入瀏覽器的 localStorage，只留在你這部裝置，不會傳送給我們。關於頁與本頁的中／英文切換也使用同一個本機鍵（hk-sms-csv-lang）。

要清除這些資料，可以：

  • 按該工具上的「清除資料」按鈕（例如外傭工資收據、大廈維修費分攤、分租水電費帳目、公立醫院收費估算、整個月薪計時器）
  • 在瀏覽器設定中清除此網站的 Cookie 與網站資料

短訊記帳CSV 不寫入 localStorage：關閉分頁或按清空後，貼上的文字即消失。

沒有帳戶、沒有伺服器存檔、沒有我們的分析程式

本站不設帳號、不要求登入，作者也不在伺服器保存你的工具內容。我們沒有自行安裝的分析、像素或追蹤程式。

託管服務的伺服器日誌

頁面由 GitHub Pages 與 Cloudflare Pages 託管。這些服務供應商可能保留標準伺服器日誌（例如 IP 位址、User-Agent、請求路徑與時間），用於營運、安全與濫用防護。該等日誌由託管商按各自政策處理，不是我們另行收集的分析資料。

Google AdSense 與廣告 Cookie

首頁會載入 Google AdSense 指令碼（pagead2.googlesyndication.com），網站上可能顯示廣告。

包括 Google 在內的第三方供應商會使用 Cookie，根據使用者先前造訪本網站或其他網站的紀錄投放廣告。

Google 使用廣告 Cookie，可讓 Google 及其合作夥伴根據使用者造訪本網站及／或互聯網上其他網站的情況，向使用者投放廣告。

使用者可前往 Google 廣告設定 停用個人化廣告；亦可前往 www.aboutads.info/choices 停用部分第三方供應商的 Cookie。

進一步說明見 Google： How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services。

兒童

本站並非以兒童為對象。我們不會故意收集兒童的個人資料。因運算在瀏覽器進行，我們也沒有伺服器端的兒童資料檔。

外部連結

本站可能連結至政府網站、銀行無關的說明、GitHub、Google 政策頁等。我們不負責外部網站的內容、可用性或私隱做法。點擊離開本站後，請參閱該網站自己的政策。

政策修訂

我們可能不時更新本政策（例如新增工具或託管安排有變）。請以本頁標示的生效日期為準。重大變更時，我們會更新此日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026 (2026-10-06)

How data is processed

This site is static pages. SMS text, names, amounts, bills and unit details you type are processed in your browser. We have no application server that receives or stores that content.

Local storage

Some tools may optionally save a draft or language choice in the browser’s localStorage. It stays on this device and is never sent to us. The Chinese / English toggle on About and this page also uses a local key (hk-sms-csv-lang).

To clear it:

  • Use the tool’s “Clear data” button (helper wage receipt, building repair levy, shared utility account, public hospital fees, monthly salary timer)
  • Clear this site’s cookies and site data in your browser settings

The SMS-to-CSV tool does not write localStorage. Closing the tab or pressing Clear removes the pasted text.

No accounts, no server-side storage, no analytics by us

There are no accounts and no sign-in. The site author does not keep a server copy of your tool input. We do not run our own analytics, pixels or trackers.

Hosting provider logs

Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages. Those providers may keep standard server logs (for example IP address, User-Agent, request path and time) for operations, security and abuse prevention. Those logs are handled under each provider’s own policy. They are not analytics we collect separately.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

The homepage loads the Google AdSense script (pagead2.googlesyndication.com). Ads may appear on the site.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info/choices.

See also Google’s How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Children

This site is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because processing happens in the browser, we also have no server-side children’s data files.

External links

We may link to government sites, GitHub, Google policy pages and similar. We are not responsible for the content, availability or privacy practices of those sites. After you leave, their own policies apply.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy (for example when a tool or hosting arrangement changes). The effective date at the top of this page is the current version. We will update that date when the change is material.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open a GitHub issue at https://github.com/brianlam1021/hk-sms-csv/issues
