關於本站

這是一套免費、私隱優先、只在你瀏覽器內運作的香港生活小工具。沒有帳戶、沒有上傳、沒有要安裝的 App。

這是甚麼

香港生活工具把幾件香港日常會用到的計算與文件工作，做成靜態網頁：把銀行簽賬短訊整理成 CSV、列印外傭糧單、按業權份數分攤大維修、為劏房或合租寫水電分攤帳目、估算公立醫院收費。

網站以開源靜態頁發布，部署在 GitHub Pages 與 Cloudflare Pages，原始碼在 GitHub。

給誰用

給需要自己動手、又不想把個人或財務資料交給雲端服務的香港居民，例如：

  • 想把信用卡簽賬短訊匯入試算表的持卡人
  • 要每月列印外傭工資收據的僱主
  • 要估算或通知各戶維修徵款的業主立案法團司庫、管理處或業主
  • 要為劏房規管租賃或合租室友準備水電分攤書面帳目的業主與租客
  • 想對照2026年醫管局急症室、住院或門診收費的病人與家屬

工具怎樣運作

每個工具都是瀏覽器內的靜態頁。你輸入的姓名、金額、短訊或帳單數字只在你這部裝置上運算。本站不設帳號，也不把內容上傳到我們的伺服器。部分工具可選擇把草稿或語言偏好寫入瀏覽器的 localStorage，方便下次開啟；那些資料不會送到我們這裡。詳見 私隱政策。

所有工具

  • 短訊記帳CSV — 把滙豐、恒生、中銀、渣打的信用卡簽賬短訊整理成摘要，並下載 UTF-8 BOM CSV。
  • 外傭工資收據 — 中英雙語外傭糧單產生器，可列印或另存 PDF，並記下休息日與勞工假。
  • 大廈維修費分攤 — 按公契或業權份數把大維修開支攤分至各戶，可產生分期與繳款通知。
  • 分租水電費帳目 — 為劏房規管租賃或合租室友產生水電、煤氣與上網等費用的書面分攤帳目。
  • 公立醫院收費估算 — 按2026年1月起醫管局收費，估算急症室、住院與門診費用，並對照全年1萬元上限。

About this site

A collection of free, privacy-first, browser-only Hong Kong life-utility tools. No account, no upload, nothing to install.

What this is

HK life-utility tools are static pages for everyday Hong Kong tasks: turn bank card SMS into a CSV, print a domestic-helper wage receipt, split a building repair levy by shares, write a utility-apportionment account for an SDU or flatshare, and estimate public hospital fees after the 2026 HA reform.

The site is open-source static HTML, hosted on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages. Source: GitHub.

Who it is for

People in Hong Kong who want to do the work themselves without sending personal or financial data to a cloud service, including:

  • Cardholders who want SMS spending notices in a spreadsheet
  • Employers who print a monthly foreign domestic helper wage receipt
  • OC treasurers, management offices or owners estimating or notifying a repair levy
  • Landlords and tenants preparing a written utility-apportionment account for a regulated SDU tenancy or flatshare
  • Patients and families checking 2026 Hospital Authority A&E, inpatient or clinic charges

How the tools are built

Every tool runs in your browser. Names, amounts, SMS text and bill figures stay on this device. There is no account and we do not upload your content to our servers. Some tools may optionally save a draft or language choice in the browser’s localStorage; that data is never sent to us. See the Privacy Policy.

All tools

  • SMS ledger CSV — Parse HSBC, Hang Seng, BOC and Standard Chartered card-spending SMS into a summary and a UTF-8 BOM CSV.
  • Helper wage receipt — Bilingual FDH wage receipt: print or save PDF, plus a simple rest-day / statutory-holiday log.
  • Building repair levy — Apportion major-repair costs by DMC or undivided shares, with instalments and payment notices.
  • Shared utility account — Written apportionment account for SDU regulated tenancies or flatmates (electricity, water, gas, broadband).
  • Public hospital fees — Estimate HA A&E, inpatient and clinic charges from 1 January 2026, including the HK$10,000 annual cap.
